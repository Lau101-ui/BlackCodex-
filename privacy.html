Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2026

Draft for review. Check it matches how you actually run the business, then delete this note. This is a starting point, not legal advice.

The short version

This website does not use cookies, analytics, advertising trackers or user accounts. It has no database. We do not collect or store information about you when you browse it.

The booking form

The form on the contact page builds a message on your own device. When you choose "Send on WhatsApp" or "Send by email", your phone or computer opens WhatsApp or your email app with the message ready. The message is only sent if you send it. Nothing you type in the form is sent to or stored on this website.

What happens after you message us

If you contact us by WhatsApp, email or phone, we receive your name, contact details and what you tell us. We use this to reply, to quote, to plan and deliver the work, and to keep a record of the booking. We do not sell or share it, except where a person helping on your job, such as our photography partner, needs it to do that job. WhatsApp and your email provider handle those messages under their own privacy policies.

Fonts, scripts and videos

Fonts and scripts on this site are served from the site itself, so your visit is not shared with font or script providers. Where a project page shows a video, it may load from YouTube's privacy-enhanced domain (youtube-nocookie.com) only after you open that project. That video is then handled under YouTube's own policies.

Hosting

The site is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub, like any web host, may keep technical records such as IP addresses in its server logs. We do not have access to a visitor list.

Your rights

You can ask us what we hold about you, ask us to correct it, or ask us to delete it, subject to any legal duty to keep records. We will handle your request in line with the data protection law that applies in Malawi.
